Disclaimer

This app generates router templates. It is not a structural design tool: it does not check whether a joint can carry the loads in your structure. Its warnings only cover geometry and machining.

Load-bearing connections must be designed and checked by a qualified structural engineer according to the applicable building codes (e.g. Eurocode 5). Wood quality, moisture, fit, fasteners and workmanship all affect the strength of a joint.

All results, drawings and files are provided as is, without any warranty of correctness or fitness for a particular purpose. You use them at your own risk.
