## Title

Router templates for beam dovetail joints (joist into main beam)

## Summary

Router templates for big dovetail joints between wooden beams: a socket template
for the side of the main beam and a tenon template for the end of the joist.
Seven joist sizes from 60 × 120 to 120 × 240 mm. Other sizes, bits and copy
rings: use the free online generator.

## Description

Cut strong, hidden dovetail joints between a joist and a main beam with a
hand-held router — for pergolas, carports, garden sheds, roof and floor
structures. The joist drops in from above and the slightly tapered flanks
pull the joint tight.

Each set has two templates:

- **Socket template** — screwed to the side face of the main beam; stop lips
  hook over the top face.
- **Tenon template** — screwed to the end grain of the joist; the screw holes
  end up hidden inside the joint.

**Required tools** (all sets):

- dovetail bit Ø 25.4 mm (1"), 14°, at least 21 mm cutting length
- template guide / copy ring Ø 30 mm with a collar shorter than 8 mm
- router that takes that copy ring

Bit depth below the router base: 29 mm for the socket, 28 mm for the tenon.

**Different bit, copy ring, beam size or fit?** The free generator makes
templates for any combination, shows the joint in 3D and also exports 1:1 PDFs
for templates too large to print:
https://patrick-praher.github.io/beam-dovetail-templates/

**How to use**

1. Remove most of the waste first (saw, drill or straight bit).
2. Fix the template, centre notch on your layout line.
3. Start with the bit outside the wood, cut in, come back out the same way.
   A dovetail bit cannot plunge — never lift the router in the cut.
4. Always make a test joint in scrap first.

These templates are geometry, not structural engineering. Have load-bearing
joints dimensioned by someone qualified.

## Print settings

- Orientation: as exported — router face on the bed, lips pointing up.
  No supports.
- Material: PETG or ASA preferred; PLA works but softens near a hot bit.
- 4+ walls, 30–40 % infill, 0.2 mm layers.
- Largest part: 225 mm — fits 256 mm beds (Bambu X1/P1/A1). The three smallest
  tenon templates with support frame are 245 mm tall.
